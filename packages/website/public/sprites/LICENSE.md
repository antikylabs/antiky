# Sprite asset licenses

Every sprite sheet in this directory is available under
[Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The character
sources declare CC0 on their OpenGameArt pages. Kenney additionally confirms that assets
published on its asset pages are public-domain licensed and may be used commercially without
attribution.

Attribution is not required. Antiky Labs credits the artists voluntarily.
