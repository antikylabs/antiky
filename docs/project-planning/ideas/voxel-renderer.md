I absolutely loved https://ephtracy.github.io/index.html?page=mv_main back in the day and the rendering capabilities it was able to do (Just look at the examples and at his twitter etc!)...

I want to do this on webgpu, super awesome shader rendering of voxel.

Some great examples of how far the style can go are [Deep in the Woods](https://www.reddit.com/r/Art/comments/dfgbia/deep_in_the_woods_me_voxelart_2019/), the animated [Tokyo suburb](https://www.behance.net/gallery/55316023/Tokyo-suburb-Voxel-Art-Animation), and [The Fox](https://www.behance.net/gallery/111881053/The-Fox). They range from dense, atmospheric scenes to small animated dioramas and graphic illustration, and were all made with MagicaVoxel.

The natural interchange format is MagicaVoxel's documented [`.vox` format](https://github.com/ephtracy/voxel-model), including its scene graph and material extensions. The closest prior art is Addison Prairie's open-source [WebGPU `.vox` path tracer](https://github.com/AddisonPrairie/WebGPU-.vox) and [live demo](https://addisonprairie.github.io/WebGPU-.vox/); [Voxel Builder](https://github.com/nimadez/voxel-builder) and the community [MagicaVoxel shader collection](https://github.com/lachlanmcdonald/magicavoxel-shaders) are useful surrounding work.
