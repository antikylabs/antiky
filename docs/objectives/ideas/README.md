# Ideas

Opportunities worth recording but **not worth building yet**.

An idea lives here when it is plausibly valuable and clearly premature. Recording it stops the
thinking being lost, and keeps it out of the active objectives where it would compete with work
that is actually ready.

An idea leaves this folder in one of three ways: it becomes an objective, it becomes an AIP when it
needs alignment before implementation, or it gets deleted with a note saying why it was wrong.
Nothing here is a commitment, and nothing here should be cited as authority — an ADR may not use a
planning document as its basis, and these are less than plans.
